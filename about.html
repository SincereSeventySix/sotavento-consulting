About

An independent firm, built to sit on the client’s side of the table.

Capital raising, debt advisory, and transaction execution for sponsors, developers, and operating businesses.

The practice

Sotavento Consulting is an independent advisory and brokerage firm. We structure and place capital for growth, development, acquisitions, and refinancing.

The work spans real estate, hospitality, logistics, infrastructure, and SMEs. Advice and brokerage run across the full capital stack — senior and mezzanine debt, structured finance, and project finance — tailored to each client’s objectives and risk profile.

With more than forty years of combined financial and project experience, the firm has executed complex financing mandates across East and West Africa. We combine conflict-free advice with active brokerage, so a mandate can move from capital strategy and structuring to lender and investor engagement, negotiation, and financial close.

The network includes institutional lenders, development finance institutions, and private equity partners, with direct access to senior decision-makers.

As the sole independent wealth and asset management firm in Cabo Verde, and the first to introduce private brokerage solutions in that market, Sotavento pairs institutional execution with regional insight.

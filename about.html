About

An independent firm for capital raising, debt advisory, and execution.

We partner with sponsors, developers, and operating businesses to structure and place capital for growth, development, acquisitions, and refinancing.

The company

Sotavento Consulting is an independent advisory and brokerage firm providing capital raising, debt advisory, and transaction execution services across Africa.

The work spans energy — wind, solar, and water infrastructure — hospitality, logistics, real estate, and SMEs. Advice runs across the full capital stack, from senior and mezzanine debt to structured and project finance, EPC+F partner connections, and pre-project finance for feasibility and environmental studies.

With more than forty years of combined experience, the firm has been Pan-African since 2017. As the sole independent wealth and asset management firm in Cabo Verde, and the first to introduce private brokerage solutions in that market, Sotavento pairs institutional execution with regional insight.

Hospitality

Hotels, aparthotels, and mixed-use

Sotavento connects hotel and resort developers to international brand operators, structures debt and equity across the capital stack, and negotiates development concessions with government and private landholders. Project countries: Cabo Verde, Zimbabwe, and Kenya.

On the Praia waterfront, the KK Eurostars Hotel and Aparthotel is a €46.25 million flagship — 65% debt and 35% equity, brand-operated by Accor with JS-CV, and about 2,700 jobs across development and operation. Sotavento’s role: capital structuring, EPC+F connection, and brand-operator placement.

Leadership

A small partnership, with the reach of a much larger one.

Leadership in Praia, East Africa, and Asia.

  • Piran Johnson

    Chief Executive Officer

    As CEO of Sotavento Consulting, Piran leads the firm’s strategic expansion across debt advisory, project implementation advisory, and fixed income and equities brokerage. He supports projects and businesses in real estate, hospitality, logistics, renewable infrastructure and energy, commercial projects, and SMEs. He has over 25 years of professional experience across the African continent, including multi-million-dollar international projects. He holds a B.A. (Hons) from the University of Liverpool and a certification in Hotel Finance and Asset Management from Cornell University.

  • Wilfred Musau

    Senior Partner, East Africa

    Wilfred Musau is a seasoned banking and finance executive from Kenya. He has held senior leadership positions at major financial institutions, including director roles and Managing Director & CEO of the National Bank of Kenya. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Banking and Finance from Kenyatta University and advanced banking and strategy training from the University of Stellenbosch Business School. His experience spans commercial banking leadership, corporate financing, and institutional partnerships across East Africa.

  • S. Filomeno Monteiro

    Asian Regional Director

    S. Filomeno Monteiro has spent 35 years living and working across Asia in technology and business. He is fluent in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, and Portuguese, and is a graduate of the University of New Brunswick, Canada. At Sotavento he supports projects and operating businesses across sectors, bringing technology, market access, and cross-border judgement to the mandate.

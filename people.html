Leadership in Praia, East Africa, and Asia.

  • Piran Johnson

    Chief Executive

    Piran leads the firm’s work across debt advisory, project implementation advisory, and fixed income and equities brokerage. He has more than 18 years of professional experience across the continent, including multi-million international projects in real estate, hospitality, logistics, renewable infrastructure, commercial projects, and SMEs. He holds a B.A. (Hons) from the University of Liverpool and a certification in Hotel Finance and Asset Management from Cornell University.

  • Wilfred Musau

    Senior Partner, East Africa

    Wilfred Musau is a banking and finance executive from Kenya. He has held senior leadership positions at major financial institutions, including director roles and Managing Director & CEO of the National Bank of Kenya. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Banking and Finance from Kenyatta University and completed advanced banking and strategy training at the University of Stellenbosch Business School. His work spans commercial banking leadership, corporate financing, and institutional partnerships across East Africa.

  • S. Filomeno Monteiro

    Asian Regional Director

    S. Filomeno Monteiro has spent 35 years living and working across Asia in technology and business. He is fluent in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, and Portuguese, and is a graduate of the University of New Brunswick, Canada. At Sotavento he supports projects and operating businesses across sectors, bringing technology, market access, and cross-border judgement to the mandate.

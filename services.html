Human resources

An outsourced human resources desk for corporate needs: liability kept in check, and the administration actually finished. Built for companies operating in Cabo Verde that do not want to staff a full internal function on day one.

  • Recruitment and placement
  • Policies and procedures
  • Employee engagement, mentoring, and coaching
